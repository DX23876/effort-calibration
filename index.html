Share your WHOOP data to calibrate NOOP's Effort

This page turns a WHOOP data export into two small files for the Effort recalibration in ryanbr/noop#2438. It keeps only what the fit needs and drops everything personal.

Your data stays on your device

Everything runs in your browser. Nothing is uploaded: this page has no server, and its security policy blocks every network request. You can also save the page and open it offline.

Kept: activity name, duration, strain, max/avg heart rate, HR-zone shares; per day: Day Strain, resting/max/avg heart rate. Days are numbered 0, 1, 2… instead of dated.
Dropped: all dates and times, sleep, HRV, recovery, skin temperature, SpO₂ and the journal.

1Get your WHOOP export

Request your data export in the WHOOP app (Data Export). WHOOP sends you a ZIP file. You don't need to unzip it.

2Choose the ZIP file
